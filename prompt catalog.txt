Build a dedicated Master Product Catalog Module specifically for Provision Shops.

This module will serve as the central database of products that all provision shops on AkuapemConnect can use.

The objective is to eliminate duplicate product creation by allowing shops to simply select existing products from the catalog instead of creating them from scratch.

Only administrators can create, edit and manage the Master Catalog.

--------------------------------------------------
BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------

Create one central product catalog for common grocery and provision products.

Every shop shares the same catalog.

Each shop only manages its own inventory, pricing and stock.
