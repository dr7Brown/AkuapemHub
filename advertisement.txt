====================================================================
AKUAPEMCONNECT — 5 x 30-SECOND AD SCRIPTS
====================================================================
Ready-to-record voice-over scripts (~80-95 words each, timed for a
30-second read at a natural pace). Suitable for radio, WhatsApp
status/video, or as a voice-over track under a short video ad.
Open in Word to edit, adjust names/URLs, and format for your VO
talent or video editor. Replace [BRACKETS] before recording.

Each script includes: target audience, VO copy, and basic
production cues ([MUSIC], [SFX], [PAUSE]) you can keep or strip out.


--------------------------------------------------------------------

====================================================================
PRODUCTION NOTES
====================================================================
  - Read each script aloud once with a stopwatch before recording —
    natural pace varies by voice talent; trim or expand slightly to
    land at 28-30 seconds.
  - Replace [website URL] consistently across all five scripts.
  - Scripts 2-5 can double as short vertical video ads (WhatsApp
    Status, Instagram/TikTok) by pairing the VO with matching
    b-roll: Script 2 = workers at work, Script 3 = a happy customer,
    Script 4 = a shop owner packing an order, Script 5 = a durbar/
    community gathering.
  - Consider recording Script 1 (general) as the primary radio spot,
    and Scripts 2-4 as targeted follow-ups once initial awareness is
    built.
