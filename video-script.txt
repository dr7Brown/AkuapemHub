====================================================================
AKUAPEMCONNECT — 30-SECOND APP OVERVIEW VIDEO SCRIPT
====================================================================
Format: Shot-by-shot video script (visuals + VO + music/SFX cues),
timed to a 30-second cut. Set in everyday Akuapem/Ghanaian life —
market, workshop, home, phone-in-hand moments. Open in Word to edit;
replace [BRACKETS] before shooting/editing. Read the VO aloud once
with a stopwatch — trim slightly to land at 28-30 seconds.

Tone: Warm, energetic, fast-paced, proud-of-community.
Music: Upbeat highlife/Afrobeat instrumental bed under the whole video,
swelling on the final logo card.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
SHOT LIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------

[0:00–0:03] — SCENE 1: THE HOOK
VISUAL: Wide shot of a busy Akuapem street/market at golden hour —
trotro passing, a trader arranging tomatoes, a carpenter sanding wood
outside his shop. Energetic, colourful, real.
VO: "Akuapem is full of talent, business... and busy people."
[MUSIC IN: highlife instrumental, upbeat]

[0:03–0:07] — SCENE 2: THE PROBLEM
VISUAL: Quick cuts — a woman looking frustrated at a leaking tap; a
young man scrolling his phone, unsure where to find work; a shop
owner staring at empty street traffic outside her store.
VO: "Finding help. Finding work. Finding customers — it isn't always
easy."
[SFX: dripping tap, brief]

[0:07–0:10] — SCENE 3: THE TURN / APP REVEAL
VISUAL: Phone screen lights up, AkuapemConnect logo animates in over
a hand holding the phone, community/market blurred in background.
VO: "Meet AkuapemConnect — everything Akuapem needs, in one app."
[MUSIC: lifts a notch]

[0:10–0:22] — SCENE 4: FEATURE MONTAGE (fast cuts, ~2s each)
VISUAL/VO pairs, quick screen-recording + real-life cutaways:
  • Electrician fixing a socket, customer smiling, phone showing a
    5-star rating — VO: "Hire trusted local workers—"
  • Phone screen: tapping "Post a Job" — VO: "—or post a job in
    seconds."
  • Trader packing a parcel, delivery rider on a motorbike — VO:
    "Buy and sell on the Marketplace, with delivery to your door."
  • Phone screen: News feed / event flyer / funeral notice scrolling
    — VO: "Stay updated with local news, events, and announcements."
  • Two people shaking hands / worker and customer chatting on phone
    chat screen — VO: "Chat safely. Pay safely. Get it done."
[MUSIC: energetic, driving under the montage]

[0:22–0:27] — SCENE 5: COMMUNITY PAYOFF
VISUAL: Group of diverse people — worker, trader, customer, rider —
smiling, phones in hand, community gathering feel (durbar drums
faint in background, optional).
VO: "One app. One community. Built for Akuapem, by Akuapem."
[MUSIC: swells]

[0:27–0:30] — SCENE 6: LOGO CARD / CTA
VISUAL: Clean end card — AkuapemConnect logo, website URL, App
Store/Play Store badges if available.
VO: "Download AkuapemConnect today — akuapemconnect.com"
ON-SCREEN TEXT: "AkuapemConnect — Connecting Communities. Creating
Opportunities."
[MUSIC: final hit and out]


--------------------------------------------------------------------
FULL VO SCRIPT (read straight through, ~85 words / ~28-30s)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
"Akuapem is full of talent, business... and busy people. Finding
help. Finding work. Finding customers — it isn't always easy. Meet
AkuapemConnect — everything Akuapem needs, in one app. Hire trusted
local workers, or post a job in seconds. Buy and sell on the
Marketplace, with delivery to your door. Stay updated with local
news, events, and announcements. Chat safely. Pay safely. Get it
done. One app. One community. Built for Akuapem, by Akuapem.
Download AkuapemConnect today — akuapemconnect.com."


--------------------------------------------------------------------
PRODUCTION NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  - If filming real B-roll isn't possible in time, screen-recordings
    of the actual app (jobs list, marketplace, chat, news feed) can
    replace any "phone screen" cutaway — see steps.txt for exactly
    what each flow looks like to record.
  - Keep Scene 4's cuts SHORT (under 2.5s each) — this is the part
    that sells the "one app does it all" idea, pacing matters more
    than detail here.
  - Vertical (9:16) cut works for WhatsApp Status/Instagram/TikTok;
    reframe Scene 1 and Scene 5 wide shots accordingly, or shoot both
    orientations if possible.
  - [App Store/Play Store badges] — only include in Scene 6 if the
    app actually has a published mobile app listing; otherwise keep
    it to the website URL only.
