WHAT IS AKUAPEMCONNECT?
------------------------
AkuapemConnect is a live, working digital platform serving the towns
and people of the Akuapem area. It is a single, trusted, mobile-
friendly place where residents find jobs, buy and sell goods, arrange
local delivery, and stay connected through community news, events,
and funeral announcements.


THE PROBLEM
------------
Local economic and community activity across Akuapem is scattered
and hard to discover. Skilled workers struggle to be found; local
traders have limited visibility; job seekers rely on word of mouth;
and community news and funeral announcements often miss the people
who need them most — especially relatives living outside Akuapem.


THE SOLUTION
------------
AkuapemConnect brings all of this into one platform:
  - Jobs & Services — post/find work, hire verified workers
  - Find Workers — a searchable directory of local artisans
  - Marketplace — local shops sell online; buyers order and pay
    securely
  - Delivery Services — a local rider network fulfils orders
  - Community News, Events & Funeral Announcements — reaching the
    whole community, including the diaspora
  - Built-in trust & safety: verification, secure payments, ratings


WHY IT MATTERS
---------------
  - Jobs and steady income for artisans, traders, and riders
  - Real visibility and growth for local businesses
  - A structured, dignified path to income for young people
  - Stronger community communication across Akuapem's towns
  - Digital inclusion, built for everyday non-technical users
