AKUAPEMCONNECT — POINTS & MILESTONE REWARDS STRATEGY
======================================================
Prepared as a recommendation for configuring the Referrals & Points module
(Admin → Users → Referrals) and the Milestone Reward system
(Admin → Users → Reward Milestones / Reward Claims).

MARKETING PHILOSOPHY — WHY THESE NUMBERS
------------------------------------------
1. Reward GROWTH the most. A referral who actually pays for something is
   worth more to the platform than almost any other single action — so
   referral-conversion points should sit near the top of the earning table,
   not the bottom.
2. Reward TRUST-BUILDING actions generously but ONCE. Verifying your email,
   phone, and adding a photo/ID makes every future interaction on the
   platform safer for everyone else — pay well for it, but only the first
   time (already enforced as "one-time" in the system).
3. Reward QUALITY over VOLUME on repeatable actions. Anything a user could
   spam (leaving reviews, marking jobs complete) needs a low per-action
   value AND a daily cap, so points stay a signal of real activity, not a
   grinding exploit.
4. Reward CONTENT that survives admin moderation, not content that's merely
   submitted. Points for news/events/funerals/accommodation listings should
   only fire on approval (already how it's built) — this stops low-effort
   or spam submissions from farming points.
5. Keep the TOP of the ladder rare and cash-based, and the BOTTOM of the
   ladder frequent and cheap (badges, small airtime). This makes the
   programme feel achievable early (retention) while keeping real payout
   cost concentrated on your most loyal, highest-LTV users.

PART 1 — POINTS PER ACTION
====================================================================
Format: Action | Points | Frequency | Daily Cap | Why

ACCOUNT & TRUST (one-time — pays for the safety/quality signal once)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Complete registration                      10 pts   one-time   —
Verify email address                       10 pts   one-time   —
Verify phone number                        10 pts   one-time   —
Upload a profile photo                     10 pts   one-time   —
Complete worker/ID verification badge      25 pts   one-time   —
  → Verification is the single strongest trust signal on the platform —
    price it like it matters.

REFERRALS (your growth engine — price these HIGHEST per action)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Friend registers via your link             15 pts   repeatable   —
Referred friend verifies their email       10 pts   repeatable   —
Referred friend makes their first payment  30 pts   repeatable   —
  → This is the most valuable action on the whole table. A referral who
    pays is a proven, converting customer — reward it like a marketer who
    knows customer acquisition cost.

MARKETPLACE (buying & selling)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Open a shop / list first product           10 pts   one-time     —
Complete a sale (as seller)                10 pts   repeatable   30/day
Complete a purchase (as buyer)              5 pts   repeatable   20/day
Leave a product review                      5 pts   repeatable   15/day
  → Cap sales/purchases so a seller can't wash-trade points with a
    friend's account for free rewards.

JOBS & SERVICES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Post a job request                          5 pts   repeatable   10/day
Hire a worker                              10 pts   repeatable   20/day
Mark a job completed (as client)            5 pts   repeatable   10/day
Complete a job (as worker)                 15 pts   repeatable   30/day
Leave a review                              5 pts   repeatable   10/day
Receive a 5-star rating                    10 pts   repeatable   20/day
  → Completing work (the worker side) is priced above merely hiring —
    it's the harder, more valuable half of the transaction.

COMMUNITY CONTENT (only on admin approval — never on submission)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
News article approved                      10 pts   repeatable   30/day
Event approved                             10 pts   repeatable   30/day
Funeral announcement approved               10 pts   repeatable   20/day
Accommodation listing approved              15 pts   repeatable   30/day
  → Accommodation is priced slightly higher — it's a newer module and
    you want to seed supply (listings) faster than the others right now.

PART 2 — MILESTONE POINTS & REWARDS LADDER
====================================================================
Configure each of these as a separate Milestone in Admin → Reward
Milestones. Suggested "Maximum Claims" numbers keep the top tiers scarce
and your cash payout budget predictable — raise them later once you know
your real earn-rate and conversion.

Tier 1 — 100 pts   "Welcome Aboard"
  Reward: Badge "New Member" + GHS 2 Airtime
  Max claims: unlimited
  Why: Almost anyone who verifies email+phone+photo gets here in one
  session. It's the first taste of a real reward — keep it cheap and easy.

Tier 2 — 300 pts   "Getting Started"
  Reward: GHS 5 Airtime (or 500MB Data — let users choose by creating
  two milestones at the same threshold with different reward_type, and
  treating them as one-time-each so a user can only pick one)
  Max claims: unlimited
  Why: Reachable within the first 1–2 weeks of normal use; keeps early
  momentum going before the bigger tiers feel far away.

Tier 3 — 750 pts   "Active Member"
  Reward: Badge "Active Member" + GHS 10 Airtime
  Max claims: unlimited
  Why: This is where casual users start separating from regulars —
  reward the badge for status, and the airtime to keep it worth claiming.

Tier 4 — 1,500 pts   "Community Contributor"
  Reward: GHS 20 Cash
  Max claims: unlimited (or cap at ~500 in your first quarter to test cost)
  Why: First real cash tier. Achievable by one solid referral conversion
  plus normal weekly use — a believable, motivating target.

Tier 5 — 3,000 pts   "Trusted Local"
  Reward: GHS 50 Cash
  Max claims: 200 (raise later based on budget)
  Why: Requires sustained activity or 2–3 successful referrals — this is
  your "regular" tier, the users worth retaining most.

Tier 6 — 5,000 pts   "AkuapemConnect Champion"
  Reward: GHS 100 Cash
  Max claims: 100
  Why: Matches the example from the original spec — a genuine power-user
  milestone. Cap it so early adopters don't drain the budget before you've
  seen real redemption patterns.

Tier 7 — 10,000 pts   "Community Ambassador"
  Reward: GHS 250 Cash + 30-day Featured Profile/Listing boost (OTHER
  reward_type, fulfilled by admin toggling the feature manually)
  Max claims: 30
  Why: Bundling a non-cash perk here (visibility) reduces cash cost per
  claim while still feeling premium — ambassadors get promoted, not just
  paid.

Tier 8 — 20,000 pts   "Legacy Builder"
  Reward: GHS 500 Cash + permanent "Founding Champion" badge
  Max claims: 10 (deliberately rare)
  Why: Your top-of-funnel showcase tier — a handful of visible, real
  success stories you can use in marketing ("Kwame earned GHS 500 by
  helping build the AkuapemConnect community") is worth more than the
  payout itself.

====================================================================
BUDGET SUSTAINABILITY NOTES
====================================================================
- Total possible cash exposure if every Max Claims slot above were fully
  redeemed at cash tiers only:
      1,500 × 20   (uncapped — model this against active user count)
    +   200 × 50   = GHS 10,000
    +   100 × 100  = GHS 10,000
    +    30 × 250  = GHS  7,500
    +    10 × 500  = GHS  5,000
    -----------------------------
    ≈ GHS 32,500 + whatever Tier 4 costs at your real user count
  Start Max Claims conservative (as above), watch real redemption speed
  in Admin → Reward Claims stats for a month, then raise the caps.
- Airtime/data tiers (1–3) are cheap per unit but should still get a
  soft internal budget alert once monthly spend crosses a threshold you
  pick — they scale with total user count, not just power users.
- Review the "Points Redeemed" stat on Admin → Reward Claims monthly
  against actual GHS paid out, to catch any reward_value misconfiguration
  early.

====================================================================
HOW TO CONFIGURE THIS IN THE APP
====================================================================
1. Points per action → Admin → Users → Referrals → Config tab. Every
   action in Part 1 already has a matching entry there (or add
   funeral_post_approved / accommodation_post_approved as new milestones
   if not already present) — set the Points and Daily Cap fields to match
   this document.
2. Milestones → Admin → Users → Reward Milestones → Add Milestone. For
   each tier in Part 2, set Required Points, Reward Type, Reward Value,
   Reward Description, and Maximum Claims exactly as listed.
3. Review claims as they come in from Admin → Reward Claims (also
   surfaced in the "⚡ Your Queue" widget on the admin dashboard).
