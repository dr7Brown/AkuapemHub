AKUAPEMCONNECT — STEP-BY-STEP USER GUIDE
For video tutorials / social media walkthroughs
=================================================

This file outlines, in order, how to perform every major activity on the
AkuapemConnect app — starting from creating an account through to the most
advanced features (selling, delivery, etc.). Each section can be recorded as
its own short video.


1. CREATING AN ACCOUNT (REGISTRATION)
--------------------------------------
1. Visit https://akuapemconnect.com and tap "Sign Up".
2. Fill in the form: Full name, Username, Email, Password, Phone number,
   and Town (choose from the list, or select "Other" and type it in).
3. (Optional) Upload a profile photo.
4. Tap "Create account" — you're logged in immediately as a Customer.
   Registration is deliberately kept to this one short step for everyone;
   there's no ID upload or skill picking here, even if you plan to work.
5. Check your email for a verification link and click it (some actions on
   the app require a verified email before you can use them).
6. You'll land on the Community page — this is your home screen.
7. Want to earn on the app, not just hire? See Section 4 — you can set up
   a Worker profile any time after signing up, whenever you're ready.


2. LOGGING IN
--------------
1. Go to the homepage and tap "Login".
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Optionally tick "Stay logged in" to stay signed in on this device.
4. Tap Login — you're returned to the page you were trying to reach.
5. Forgot your password? Tap "Forgot password", enter your email or phone,
   enter the 6-digit code sent to you, then set a new password.


3. FINDING YOUR WAY AROUND (NAVIGATION)
-----------------------------------------
1. The Community page (home) shows tiles for every module: Jobs & Services,
   News, Events, Funeral Announcements, Find Workers, Delivery, Marketplace.
2. The bottom bar (always visible) gives quick access to: Jobs, My
   Applications, Community, Messages, and Settings.
3. The bell icon (top corner) shows your notifications.


4. BECOMING A WORKER / SETTING UP YOUR WORKER PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------------
1. Get there from Settings ("Become a worker") or by tapping the "Become a
   Worker" prompt on a job card while browsing Jobs — either takes you to
   the same short worker setup form.
2. Step 1, Identity verification: choose ID type (Ghana Card/Passport/
   Other), enter your ID number, and upload a clear photo of your ID.
   Optionally tap "Share my GPS location" here too — it helps match you
   with nearby jobs, but it's not required.
3. Step 2, Your skills: pick a category and a skill, tap "+ Add skill",
   and repeat for every skill you offer (at least one is required).
4. Tap "Create worker profile" to submit — you're switched into Worker
   mode immediately and can start applying for jobs. If a listing fee is
   active, you'll be asked to pay it before your profile becomes
   searchable.
5. Changed your mind, or don't have your ID handy right now? Tap "Skip for
   now — I'll set this up later" at any point on this form — you'll stay in
   Customer mode and can come back to it whenever you're ready.
6. If you've been a worker before and switched back to Customer mode,
   you'll see a "Welcome back" screen instead of the form — tap "Switch to
   worker mode" to reactivate your existing profile and skills instantly,
   no need to redo ID verification.
7. You can switch back to "Customer mode" any time from Settings — your
   worker profile is saved, not deleted.


5. POSTING A JOB (AS A CUSTOMER)
-----------------------------------
1. Go to Jobs → "Post a job".
2. Fill in: title, description, category, location, budget, job type
   (on-site/remote/hybrid), and whether you need one worker or several.
3. Choose a payment mode: "Direct" (you pay the worker yourself) or
   "Escrow" (AkuapemConnect holds the payment until the job is done).
4. Save as Draft, or Publish.
5. If Escrow was chosen, pay into escrow now. If a posting fee applies, pay
   that instead. If everything is free, your job goes straight to review.
6. Once an admin approves it, your job appears publicly under Open Jobs.


6. APPLYING FOR A JOB (AS A WORKER)
--------------------------------------
1. Go to Jobs to see the Open Jobs list.
2. Tap a job to view full details.
3. Tap Apply.
4. Track the status of your application anytime under "My Applications".


7. HIRING A WORKER (AS A CUSTOMER)
---------------------------------------
1. Open your job's applicants list ("Manage Applicants").
2. Move each applicant through: Under Review → Shortlisted → Interview
   Scheduled → Offered, or tap Hire directly.
3. Hiring a worker opens a chat conversation with them automatically.


8. COMPLETING A JOB, PAYING, AND RATING
-------------------------------------------
1. Worker: once the job is done, tap "Complete Job" (you may attach photos
   and notes).
2. If the job used Escrow: the customer reviews the work, then taps
   "Release Escrow" to pay the worker (or it auto-releases after a set
   number of days).
3. If the job was Direct payment: the customer pays the worker outside the
   app as agreed.
4. Customer rates the worker (1–5 stars + comment).
5. Either side can cancel an open job beforehand, or file a dispute if
   there's a problem.
6. Workers can view their full job history and earnings under "Worker
   History".


9. FINDING A WORKER DIRECTLY (WITHOUT POSTING A JOB)
--------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to "Find Workers".
2. Search or filter by skill, category, location, or sort by best match/
   rating.
3. Tap a worker to view their public profile.
4. Tap Message to start a chat directly — no job posting needed.
5. See top-ranked workers on the Leaderboard.


10. SHOPPING ON THE MARKETPLACE (AS A BUYER)
-------------------------------------------------
1. Go to Marketplace and browse or search/filter products.
2. Tap a product to view details, photos, price, and reviews.
3. Tap "Add to Cart" (or save it to your wishlist instead).
4. Go to Cart, review your items (grouped by shop), and tap Checkout.
5. Enter your delivery address and receiver details, then pay via Paystack.
6. Track your order under "My Orders" as it moves from Pending → Processing
   → Ready for Delivery → In Transit → Delivered.


11. SELLING ON THE MARKETPLACE (BECOMING A SELLER)
--------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to Seller Dashboard → set up your shop (name, description, contact,
   region, logo, banner).
2. Tap "Add Product" — fill in name, description, category, price, stock,
   condition, and upload photos.
3. Save as Draft or Submit for admin review; once approved it goes live.
4. Manage incoming orders from the Orders tab (Accept → Processing → Ready
   for Delivery, etc.).
5. Add a payout account (Mobile Money or Bank) under Payout Accounts so you
   can be paid.
6. Optionally boost a product or your whole shop for extra visibility
   (paid, by number of days).
7. Optionally subscribe to a seller plan for extra perks.
8. Your public shop page can be shared with buyers directly.


12. POSTING NEWS
------------------
1. Go to "My News" → fill in title, summary, content, and a featured image.
2. Submit — pay the posting fee if one applies, then wait for admin review.
3. Once approved, it appears on the News feed. Readers can Like, Save, and
   Comment on it.
4. Optionally pay to "Feature" your article for extra visibility for 30
   days.
5. Rejected articles can be edited and resubmitted from "My News".


13. POSTING AN EVENT
-----------------------
1. Go to "My Events" → fill in title, description, venue, GPS/maps link,
   date & time, organizer name, and ticket type (free/paid/registration).
2. Submit — pay the fee if one applies, then wait for admin review.
3. Once approved, it's published on the Events page.
4. Optionally pay to "Feature" your event for extra visibility.


14. POSTING A FUNERAL ANNOUNCEMENT
--------------------------------------
1. Go to "My Funerals" → fill in the deceased's name, gender, age, dates,
   biography, wake/burial/thanksgiving details, venue, and organizer
   contact.
2. Submit — pay the fee if one applies, then wait for admin approval.
3. Once approved, it's published on the Funerals page and can be shared.
4. Optionally pay to "Feature" the announcement for extra visibility.


15. REQUESTING A DELIVERY (AS A CUSTOMER)
-----------------------------------------------
1. Go to Delivery → "New Request".
2. Fill in pickup location/contact, drop-off location/contact, item
   description, weight (optional), proposed fee, and payment method.
3. Submit — most requests are approved automatically; some go through a
   quick admin check first.
4. Once approved, agents can accept it directly, or apply so you can pick
   one under "Rider Applications".
5. Track your delivery's progress step-by-step: Accepted → Picked Up → In
   Transit → Delivered.
6. Rate your delivery agent once it's delivered, or cancel/dispute if
   there's an issue.


16. BECOMING A DELIVERY AGENT
---------------------------------
1. Go to Delivery → "Become a Delivery Agent".
2. Fill in vehicle type & registration, license number, service area, bio,
   ID type/number, and upload your ID photo.
3. Submit — this goes to admin review (usually approved within 24 hours).
4. Once approved, go to your Agent Dashboard to see: Available jobs,
   Applications, Active deliveries, History, and Earnings.


17. WORKING AS A DELIVERY AGENT
-----------------------------------
1. From "Available", accept or apply for a delivery request.
2. Once assigned, update the status step by step: Accepted → Picked Up →
   In Transit → Delivered.
3. Each completed delivery adds a small commission to your balance — pay it
   off any time under "Pay Commission".
4. Optionally verify your identity with a selfie + documents for a
   higher-trust badge.
5. Optionally subscribe to a premium plan, or pay to "Sponsor" your listing
   so you rank higher for customers.


18. MANAGING YOUR ACCOUNT (SETTINGS)
----------------------------------------
1. Go to Settings to update your Profile (name, photo, phone, town).
2. "My Payments" shows every payment you've made on the app; unfinished
   payments can be resumed or cancelled here.
3. Change your password, or review Privacy & Security options.
4. Request account deletion (submitted for admin review, not instant).
5. Read the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, or contact Support/Help from
   here too.


19. MESSAGING, NOTIFICATIONS & REFERRALS
--------------------------------------------
1. Tap the bell icon anytime to see recent notifications, or "View all"
   for the full list.
2. Tap Messages in the bottom bar to see all your conversations.
3. Share your referral link/code from the "View & Earn" banner to earn
   points when friends join.
4. Check the Leaderboard to see top-ranked users by points.


SUGGESTED VIDEO RECORDING ORDER
-----------------------------------
1. Register as a customer, verify email, explore the Community page.
2. Post a job → pay/escrow if needed → wait for approval.
3. Become a Worker → apply to a job → get hired → complete → get rated/paid.
4. Find Workers directly and message one.
5. Marketplace as a buyer (browse → cart → checkout → track order), then
   as a seller (shop → product → orders → payouts → boost).
6. News, Events, and Funerals — post, pay (if needed), get approved,
   optionally feature.
7. Delivery as a customer (request → track), then as an agent (apply →
   deliver → earn → verify/subscribe/sponsor).
8. Settings tour: profile, payments, password, privacy, notifications,
   referrals.



For a localhost app like this, your best bet is a browser-extension 
tool that auto-captures your clicks as you walk through the script
 yourself — Scribe (scribehow.com) or Guidde (guidde.com) both do 
 this well: you perform each step from steps.txt once, and it auto-
 generates numbered screenshots/video with captions and even AI voiceover, 
 which you can then edit or re-record over.

The tradeoff: these still require you to physically click through the 
site per step — there's no reliable tool that reads a script and 
drives an unfamiliar app's UI itself (agentic browser automation 
exists but is flaky and not built for narrated tutorials). 
If you want something even simpler with zero setup, 
just screen-record with OBS or Loom while following the 
script and narrating live — less polish, but nothing to configure.

That opens up one more option: since it's a public URL now 
(not localhost), a scripted browser tool like Playwright 
could actually drive the site itself and record video for the 
simpler read-only flows (browsing jobs, marketplace, news) — 
I could write that for you. But for anything 
involving login, forms, or Paystack payment redirects, 
a scripted bot gets fragile fast (session state, OTP codes, 
real payment flows breaking automation), so full end-to-end 
tutorials are still better done by a human clicking through.

My recommendation stays Scribe or Guidde — same as before, 
just now even easier since you're hitting a real public URL 
with no localhost quirks: install the extension, 
click through each numbered step from steps.txt yourself, 
and it auto-captures screenshots/video with captions you can 
edit before publishing.
